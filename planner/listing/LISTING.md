## Description (use on both platforms)

Most budget templates make you do the adding up. This one doesn't.

Financial Planner 2027 is a 23-page editable PDF. Type your numbers and the gold boxes total themselves: monthly targets, running balances, what you saved and what you could save.

**What's inside**
- **My 2027 savings goal:** your target split into monthly, weekly and daily amounts, with milestone rewards
- **Money snapshot:** what you own and owe, January vs December
- **Subscription audit:** tick what you didn't use, tick what to cancel, see the yearly saving
- **Spending leaks:** shopping, takeaways and impulse buys, with targets you can live with
- **Idle money check:** spot cash earning almost nothing and see what a better rate adds
- **Owed to me:** log every IOU, plus gentle, clear and firm reminder scripts
- **Savings tracker:** fills in from your monthly pages
- **My earnings 2027:** every income source, month by month, and the % you saved
- **12 monthly budgets:** 6 income lines, 14 spending categories, planned vs actual, a no-spend calendar
- **Year in review:** your year in totals, plus reflection prompts

**Made for real life**
- **Any currency:** $, €, £, ¥, S$, A$, RM, Rs, AED and more, or type your own
- **Side income? Covered.** Tick income with no tax taken out and the planner sets your chosen % aside every month
- **A4 and US Letter** both included
- Built on take-home pay, so it works whatever your country's tax rules

**How to use**
1. Download the two PDF files.
2. Open one in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader (Windows, Mac, iPad, Android).
3. Pick your currency, type, tick and save.

**Please note**
- Automatic totals need a PDF app that supports form calculations, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. In some browsers and basic phone viewers you can type in every box, but totals won't update.
- Digital download, for personal use. Please don't resell or share the file.
- Educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

## FAQ

**Can I use it on my phone or iPad?**
Yes, with the free Adobe Acrobat Reader app. Some built-in phone viewers let you type but won't total.

**Does it calculate my tax?**
No. Tax rules differ by country, so the planner uses take-home pay. For side income with no tax taken out, you pick a % to set aside and it reserves that for you.

**Can I print it?**
Yes. Both sizes print cleanly at home.

Yes. Past buyers get a discount code.
