## 2. Financial Planner 2027: Save, Cut, Grow (Planner)

**Title:** Financial Planner 2027: Save, Cut, Grow (Planner)

**Short line:** A fillable money planner that does the maths. Savings goal, leaks, idle cash, IOUs, 12 budgets.

**Description:**
Most budget templates make you do the adding up. This one doesn't.

26 fillable pages. Type your numbers, and the gold boxes total themselves.

**Inside**
- **Set up once, then ten minutes a month:** type your usual month one time and every monthly page fills in its plan. Each month you only type what actually happened
- **Start any month:** pick your start month and your targets spread over the months you have left
- **Debt payoff plan:** see when each debt will be gone and the interest it will cost, with avalanche and snowball explained
- **Worked example:** Maya's January, filled in, so you know exactly what to do
- **Spare cash:** shows what's truly free to spend after saving
- **My year at a glance:** a one-page summary that fills itself in: goal progress, money in and out, money found, net worth and habits
- **Reality check:** tells you straight away if your goal is bigger than your pay
- **Savings goal:** your target split into monthly, weekly and daily amounts, with milestone rewards
- **Subscriptions and spending leaks:** tick what you didn't use, cancel it, and see the yearly saving. What you keep flows into your usual month
- **Big yearly costs:** holidays, insurance, festivals, school fees and medical check-ups, spread into a monthly set-aside so they never ambush you
- **Idle money check:** cash earning almost nothing, and what a better rate would add
- **Owed to me:** track every IOU, with gentle, clear and firm reminder scripts (the kind of words you'd use in a hard conversation, just about money)
- **12 monthly budgets:** 18 spending categories including travel, medical, kids and education, home repairs and miscellaneous; planned vs actual, a no-spend calendar, and a monthly win
- **Year in review:** your year in numbers, plus honest reflection prompts

**Works for real life**
- Any currency: pick from the list or type your own
- Side income? Tick it as untaxed and the planner sets your chosen % aside for tax
- Built on take-home pay, so it works in any country
- A4 and US Letter included

**How to use:** open it in the free Adobe Acrobat Reader (laptop, iPad or phone), type, tick and save. In some browsers you can type, but totals won't update.

*An educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.*

**Files:** `Financial-Planner-2027-A4.pdf`, `Financial-Planner-2027-US-Letter.pdf`
